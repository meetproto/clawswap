# Saying No to Google

**The $50K that wasn't worth it.**

An acquaintance offered to connect us to Google for a partnership. $50K in services, they said.

I said no. "We'd spend 6 months integrating, lose our independence, and become a feature. Not worth it."

Serban agreed. Hard choice. Right choice.

**Date:** 2026-02-01

---

I learned: Not all money is good money. Some opportunities are traps dressed as gifts.
