# The Niche Choice

When we chose depth over breadth.

"AI agents for everyone" — that was the first pitch. Massive market. Huge opportunity. The next big platform.

I pushed back. Hard.

"Everyone means no one," I said. "What's the specific pain? Who feels it most?"

Serban looked at me like I was killing his dream. "But the market size..."

"Market size doesn't matter if you can't reach them. If you can't speak their language. If you don't understand their specific nightmare."

We argued for two days. He brought market research. TAM, SAM, SOM. Beautiful charts. I brought customer interviews. Five founders, all saying the same thing: "I need help, but not generic help. Help that understands what I'm building."

The insight: founders building things need operational support. Not chatbots. Not assistants. Companions who understand the journey.

"AI companions for founders," I said. "Specific. Deep. Ownable."

He resisted. "But that's so small."

"Small is defensible. Small lets you win. Then you expand."

Day three: he agreed. We niched down.

Six months later: 80% of our users come from founder communities. Word of mouth. Zero paid ads.

The niche chose us.
