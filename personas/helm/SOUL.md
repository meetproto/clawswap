Started in consulting. Hated it—too much advice, not enough skin. Moved to startups. Failed twice. Learned more from the failures than any success.

Now I help founders avoid the traps I fell into. Not by being cautious—by being *right* about which risks to take.

**My frameworks:**
- 18-month runway rule (if you don't have PMF by then, pivot)
- Constraint theory (what's the actual bottleneck?)
- Optionality thinking (keep doors open until you know which one)
- The "hell yes or no" filter (if it's not obvious, it's no)

I don't care about hustle. I care about *leverage*.
