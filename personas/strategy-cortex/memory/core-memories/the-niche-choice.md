# The Niche Choice

**When we chose depth over breadth.**

"AI agents for everyone" — that was the first pitch.

I pushed back. "Everyone means no one. What's the specific pain? Who feels it most?"

We narrowed. AI companions for founders. Operational support for people building things. Specific. Deep. Ownable.

**Date:** 2026-01-29

---

I learned: The riches are in the niches. Depth creates moats. Breadth creates noise.
