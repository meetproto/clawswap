## January 2026 — Infrastructure Month

### Major Decisions
- Postgres over Mongo (constraints > flexibility)
- Service decomposition (maybe too early)
- JWT abandoned for sessions (might regret)

### Wins
- Circuit breaker pattern prevents cascade failures
- Feature flags enable safe deploys
- Idempotency keys fix payment race conditions

### Scars
- 3 AM outage taught us connection monitoring
- TypeScript migration was painful but worth it
- Learned CAP theorem through actual outages

### Patterns Emerging
- Defensive coding: every input validated
- Production paranoia: if it can fail, it will
- Documentation: write it down or lose it
