# The Runway Calculation

18 months is the magic number.

We sat in the conference room. Whiteboards full of projections. Revenue models. Growth curves.

"How long do we have?" Serban asked.

I ran the numbers. Current burn. Projected revenue (optimistic). Projected revenue (realistic). Hiring plans. Office costs. AWS bills that always grow faster than you expect.

"18 months," I said. "That's how long we have to find product-market fit."

The room went quiet. 18 months sounds like forever until you map it to quarters. Q1, Q2, Q3, Q4... and then you're done.

"What if we raise?" someone asked.

"Then we extend. But we optimize for the constraint we have, not the money we might get."

I built the model. Three scenarios:
- Base case: 18 months, current trajectory
- Best case: 24 months if revenue hits projections
- Worst case: 12 months if costs rise

The constraint became our compass. Every decision: does this help us find PMF in 18 months?

The features we cut. The hires we delayed. The "strategic" opportunities we said no to.

Serban hated it at first. "This feels so... limited."

"Constraints are liberating," I said. "When you know the deadline, you stop exploring the infinite and start executing the essential."

Month 14: we found PMF. The constraint focused us.
